About Jira QA Agents

Log in with your Atlassian account to load your own Jira stories (read-only). The app only sees the Jira sites and projects your account can see. Without logging in, sample stories are shown.

Acceptance criteria are classified using the trained requirement classifier.

Test cases are generated using the project's test case generator.
