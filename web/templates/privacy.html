Privacy Policy

Jira QA Agents — Jira UI Automation System Using Self-Healing
Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Jira QA Agents web app ("the App", "we", "us") accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is an academic software project developed by graduation project team GP27 / SWE12 at Misr International University (MIU). It reads user stories from Atlassian Jira Cloud and generates test cases from their acceptance criteria. Users sign in with their own Atlassian account through Atlassian's OAuth 2.0 (3LO) authorization.

1. Information we access

The App requests read-only access, with the scopes read:jira-work and read:jira-user. It cannot create, edit or delete anything in your Jira site.

CategoryExamplesWhy
Jira site and projectSite (cloud) ID and name, project keys and namesTo let you choose which site and project to read
Issue contentIssue key, summary, description and acceptance-criteria fieldTo classify acceptance criteria and generate test cases
Authorization tokenOAuth access token issued by AtlassianTo call the Jira API on your behalf while you use the App

We never receive or see your Atlassian password.

2. How we use information

  • Only to provide the App's features: showing your stories, classifying acceptance criteria with a machine-learning model bundled inside the App, and generating test cases that you can view or download.
  • Your Jira content is processed only while a page request is running. It is not sent to any outside AI service.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

3. Storage and security

  • The App has no database and does not store your personal data or Jira content on its servers.
  • Your OAuth token, selected site ID and project key are kept only in your own browser's session cookie. The token is encrypted, and the cookie is HTTP-only, Secure and SameSite=Lax.
  • All traffic is sent over HTTPS (TLS). The App is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard request logs for operating the service.

4. Data sharing

Data passes only between:

  • Atlassian, to read the Jira data you authorized (Atlassian Privacy Policy).
  • Vercel, which hosts and runs the App.

5. Retention

  • Nothing is kept by the App after your request ends.
  • The session cookie, with the encrypted token, is removed when you log out, clear your browser cookies, or the token expires.

6. Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time: in your Atlassian account, open Connected apps and remove Jira QA Agents.
  • Because the App stores no personal data, there is nothing for us to delete after you revoke access.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
